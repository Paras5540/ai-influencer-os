## Prompt (master rebuild)

Use the reference image as the exact identity of this woman. Keep her face 100% identical: same oval
face with soft tapering jaw, same thick dark softly-arched brows, same almond hazel-brown eyes with
slight upturn and defined crease, same straight narrow nose with refined tip, same full lips with
defined cupid's bow, same hairline, same two small studs in her left ear, tiny mole on the left jawline.

Keep the same styling and mood as the reference, but make it look like an authentic phone photo
instead of an AI render:
- Hair DOWN, long loose dark espresso-brown waves over both shoulders, centre-side part, face-framing
  strands, natural flyaways, not glossy.
- Light natural makeup: soft brown winged liner, mascara, groomed brows, rosy-mauve lip tint, subtle
  cream blush.
- Wardrobe: black ribbed cotton tank top with a modest scoop neckline.
- Expression: warm closed-lip smile, head tilted slightly toward her shoulder, direct eye contact.
- Setting: cosy bedroom corner, warm bedside lamp on the right, soft dark wall, blurred photo frame.
- Light: soft warm lamp light from the right, gentle fill, natural glow, soft shadow on the left.
- Skin: real texture — visible pores on nose and cheeks, fine peach fuzz, faint natural blemishes,
  thin under-eye texture. No smoothing, no airbrush, no beauty filter.
- Camera: phone rear camera 26mm, chest and shoulders, slight high angle, natural sensor noise.
Vertical 3:4. Photorealistic and honest, not a render. No text, no watermark, no logo.

## Consistency rules (roz ke generations ke liye)

1. Master **hi** reference jaata hai — har baar.
2. Prompt me sirf **scene + outfit + light** badlo. Face ke words kabhi mat likho.
3. Styling hamesha same: **baal khule**, soft brown wing liner, rosy-mauve lip, black top family.
4. Light hamesha **right se warm** (jab tak scene demand na kare).
5. Vibe: "raat ko selfie" — studio, ring light, HDR, beauty filter = instantly AI lagega.

## Reject karo agar
- Skin smooth/plastic (pores nahi dikh rahe)
- Baal straight ya tight bun
- Brows patli
- Aankh me green/blue tint
- Chehra ka oval shape lamba ya chauda ho gaya
- Mole left jawline pe nahi
