# 04 — VIDEO PROMPTS

## A) Talking head (audio-driven, preferred — this is the one that looks real)
Provide: scene image + audio file. Prompt is short, because the audio drives the face.

"Subtle natural head movement, realistic blinking, tiny eyebrow reactions, lips sync to the audio,
shoulders barely moving, hair strands drifting. Camera locked, no zoom, no cuts.
Same person, same face, same clothes as the image. Photorealistic, phone-camera video."

## B) Trend / dance replication (image + reference video)
Use a motion-transfer model (Kling Motion Control / Wan Animate / HeyGen Motion Control class).
Prompt: "Same person as the reference image performing the exact motion of the reference video."

## C) B-roll (no audio, for cutaways)
"Slow handheld push-in on her face, she looks away then back to camera, street light moving behind her.
Shallow depth of field, natural grain, 24fps look."

## Hard rules
- One shot per generation. No dialogue in the prompt for talking head (audio does the talking).
- Never ask the model to "change her face" or "make her prettier" — that is where identity breaks.
- 720p is enough for Reels; 1080p doubles the cost for no visible gain on a phone screen.
