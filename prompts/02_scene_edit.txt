# 02 — SCENE EDIT TEMPLATE (used for every single post)

Always send `assets/character/master.png` as the reference image.
Never describe the face in the prompt — instead say "same woman as the reference image, same face, do not change her face".

---

## Template

Using the reference image as the exact identity of the woman, keep her face, facial features, skin tone,
hairline and the beauty spot on her left cheekbone 100% unchanged. Change only the following:

- SCENE: {scene}
- OUTFIT: {outfit}
- EXPRESSION: {expression}
- CAMERA: {camera}
- LIGHT: {light}
- MOOD / GRADE: {grade}

Photorealistic, phone-camera realism, visible skin texture, natural imperfect hair strands,
no beauty retouching, no airbrushing, no plastic skin, vertical 9:16 framing with headroom for
on-screen captions at the bottom third. No text, no watermark, no logo.

---

## Good vs bad examples

BAD:  "a beautiful Indian girl in a cafe"        -> new face every time
GOOD: "same woman as reference, now standing in a Bandra cafe queue, holding an iced coffee"

BAD:  "smiling"                                   -> changes jawline and eye shape
GOOD: "same slight closed-mouth smile as reference, chin slightly lowered"

## Rule of thumb
Every word you add about the FACE increases the chance of drift. Describe only clothes, place,
light, pose and camera. The reference image carries the identity.
